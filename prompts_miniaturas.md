# Prompts reutilizables para miniaturas — Archivos Oscuros

Uso: copia el prompt, pega el título del video donde dice `{TÍTULO}` (en español).
La IA deduce el personaje/escena a partir del título. Mismo prompt para todos los
videos, solo cambia el título.

Recomendado: IA fuerte en texto (Ideogram, GPT-image/DALL·E, Nano Banana Pro).
En la miniatura usa un gancho corto de 3-5 palabras, no el título completo.

---

## YouTube (16:9 — más elaborada)

```
Cinematic YouTube thumbnail, 16:9 aspect ratio, ultra-detailed, dark
"dark-history" documentary style.

Read this video title and infer the historical figure, era and scene it
refers to, then depict it:
"{TÍTULO}"

Composition: powerful close-up of the main warrior/figure implied by the
title, intense dramatic expression facing the camera. Chiaroscuro lighting,
warm rim light against a dark desaturated background, floating dust and
embers, subtle fog, volumetric god rays. Cinematic color grade (deep teal
shadows, warm amber highlights), high contrast, shallow depth of field, film
grain, epic and ominous mood. Leave clear empty space on one side for text.

Render the title "{TÍTULO}" in Spanish as bold condensed UPPERCASE sans-serif,
large and highly legible, white/gold with a thick dark outline, placed in the
empty space, only a few impactful words. Small "ARCHIVOS OSCUROS" wordmark in a
corner.

Photorealistic, 4K, sharp, hyper-detailed, cinematic movie-poster quality.
Avoid: cartoonish, low contrast, cluttered, watermark, distorted text.
```

## Instagram (4:5 — más llamativa, estilo IG)

```
Bold scroll-stopping Instagram thumbnail, vertical 4:5 aspect ratio, modern
high-impact style for a dark-history page.

Read this title and infer the historical subject/scene:
"{TÍTULO}"

Composition: striking centered close-up of the main figure implied by the
title, dramatic and moody but MORE saturated and graphic than a film still,
punchy vibrant contrast, a strong single-color accent glow (crimson or gold),
dark vignette, textured grunge background. Premium, cinematic yet
"thumb-stopping".

Overlay the title "{TÍTULO}" in Spanish as bold UPPERCASE modern typography,
centered or lower third, very large and legible, white with a bold accent
color and dark stroke, minimal words. Small "ARCHIVOS OSCUROS" handle at top
or bottom.

Ultra sharp, 4K, high dynamic range, dramatic, trendy, high engagement.
Avoid: dull colors, tiny text, distorted letters, watermark, clutter.
```
